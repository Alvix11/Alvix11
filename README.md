orders — reducing manual intervention entirely.
`Python` · `Odoo` · `REST API` · `XML-RPC` · `Docker`

### 🛒 Invoice Payment Report (Odoo Apps)
Custom Odoo 17 module published on the **official Odoo App Store**.
Lists all payments associated with each invoice — amount, date, journal,
and payment reference — directly from the invoice view.
Designed for accountants who need full payment traceability per invoice.
`Python` · `Odoo 17` · `PostgreSQL` · `XML` · `QWeb`
